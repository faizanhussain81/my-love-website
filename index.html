A LETTER FROM MY HEART

My Dearest

✿

Sometimes I think about all the little things you do, and I realise how much happiness they bring into my life.

Aapka roz message karna, hamara intezaar karna, aur apni asli personality hamare saamne dikhana… ye chhoti chhoti baatein hamare liye bohot maayne rakhti.

The way you wake up for Fajr, the way you worry about me, the way you wait for my messages… I notice all these things. Shayad har baar hum bol nahi paate, lekin humako aapki har ek baat mehsoos hoti.

Kabhi kabhi sochte ki Allah hamari zindagi mein aapko bhej kar kitni khoobsurat khushi diye.

Hum har namaaz mein aapke liye dua karte. Allah aapko hamesha khush rakhe, aur aapke chehre pe hamesha muskurahat rakhe.

And I want you to know one thing: chahe situation kaisi bhi ho, waqat achha ho ya mushkil, Sorry Waqt, hum aapke saath hain. Aapko kabhi akela mehsoos hone nahi denge. Hum har situation mein aapka saath dene ki poori koshish karenge.

Mohabbat karte hain hum aapse, Aur hum sirf aapse mohabbat hi nahi karte, hum aapki kaamiyabi bhi dekhna chahte. Hum chahte hain aap apni zindagi mein bohot aage badhein, apne saare khwaab poore karein. Aapki har khushi aur har kaamiyabi hamare liye bohot maayne rakhti hai.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise that I will always try my best to understand you, support you and stand beside you.

Aap hamare liye bohot khaas hain. Aur hum chahte ki aap hamesha aise hi apni asli personality ke saath rahein.

Aur haan, ek baat toh yaad rakhna… aap hamari bandi hain! ❤️

Until Our Beautiful Day Comes…

Abhi Nikah ke liye waqt hai, InshaAllah woh din bhi jald aayega. Lekin tab tak aap apna waqt achhe se guzariye. Apni family ke saath waqt spend kariye, har chhote se chhote moment ko enjoy kariye. Jo karne ka dil karta hai woh kariye, jo cheezein aapko pasand hain, jo aapke interests hain, un sab ko explore kariye.

Please don't ever feel ke aapko kisi cheez se rukna hai. We have a whole lifetime ahead of us, InshaAllah. I want you to enjoy these moments, make beautiful memories and keep smiling.

And remember, I'm always there for you. Aapko jab bhi meri zaroorat ho, hum aapke saath hain.

You are the only person I have prayed this hard for. Aapke liye humne dil se dua ki hai, aur aaj bhi har namaaz mein aapko maangte hain. Allah se bas yahi chahte ke woh aapko hamare naseeb mein behtareen tareeqe se likhe aur hamare rishte mein hamesha mohabbat, sukoon aur barkat rakhe.

The Dream We Are Waiting For…

Hamari sabse badi khwahish hai ke Nikah ke din hum aapka haath thaam kar, aapki aankhon mein dekhte hue kahen:

“Mohtarma, main qayamat tak aapka hua. Ab hamari wafa aapke naam, hamari mohabbat aapke naam, hamari har dua aapke naam… aur apni aakhri saans tak hum aapke hain.”

That day, I won't just be holding your hand. I'll be holding the hand of the person I want to spend my entire life with.

Until that beautiful day comes, just remember that you are always in my duas, always in my thoughts, and always in my heart.

Until I finally get to call you my wife, please keep being yourself, keep smiling and keep living your beautiful life. I'll be right here, cheering for you, praying for you and loving you.

With all my love,
Yours, Always. ❤️
